How to play

  • Fill the grid with 0s and 1s.
  • Each row and column must have equal numbers of 0s and 1s.
  • No more than 2 identical digits in a row or column.
  • All rows must be unique. All columns must be unique.
  • Tap a cell to cycle: empty → 0 → 1 → empty.
